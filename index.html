ABOUT SINEGUGU

Locally rooted. Operationally experienced.

Sinegugu Security CC was established in 2008 by Ntsikelelo Jeffrey Mpinda and Mfundo Thank-Lord Mbengo. Both founders began their careers as security guards, giving the company leadership grounded in frontline operational experience. The ownership structure includes Akhona Fini, who holds a 30% interest in the business.

Our services are designed around each client’s risk profile, operating environment and service requirements, supported by active supervision, disciplined personnel and responsive management.
